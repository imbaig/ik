PR6 · design review revision · proposed experience

C · Reference Desk · customer walkthrough

Review annotations stay outside the product screens. Buttons below navigate only between fixed mock states; nothing connects to an account, database or provider. Album and invoice facts use the pinned public Chinook fixture; verification, memory, loading and failure outcomes are simulated. No real support connection. This is not an application or an accessibility/visual QA pass. Direction and spec decisions, local rendered review and Imran’s closure remain pending.
